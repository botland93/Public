About

Where people, processes and digital tools meet

My background is in Communication & PR, and for more than ten years I have worked across project management and digital operations. My focus is on making everyday work clearer: improving internal processes, organizing information and helping teams use digital tools with confidence.

Today I coordinate external IT partners, administer internal systems, build reporting workflows, support websites and help move changes from a practical need to a working result.

I am most comfortable between people and technology — clarifying what is needed, coordinating the right contributors, testing the outcome and making sure it works in practice.
