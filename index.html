About

Where people, processes and digital tools meet

My background is in project management and communication. Over time, the digital side of the work became a bigger part of what I do: improving internal tools, organising data, supporting users and helping technical changes make sense in everyday practice.

Today I coordinate external IT partners, administer internal systems, build Excel-based reporting workflows, support websites and help colleagues solve practical digital problems.

I especially enjoy the part in between people and technology — understanding what is needed, turning it into a clear task, testing the result and making sure it is genuinely useful.
